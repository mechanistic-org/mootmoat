MootMoat is a DIY method for documenting engineering experience. You choose a project, describe a consequential decision, identify the supporting evidence, and make the limits visible. The resulting record belongs under your control.

The work starts with a concrete question: **what did you decide, under which constraints, and what supports your account?**

## What you make

A useful first result is one short project record containing:

- A bounded claim about your contribution.
- The constraint and alternatives that shaped the decision.
- The observation or artifact that supports the claim.
- The outcome, including what remains uncertain.
- A version suitable for the audience and permitted disclosure level.

The [evidence workbook](/docs/workbook/) gives you twelve ways to draft that record. You can use a text file or your existing writing tools. No account, special schema, or software installation is needed to use the method.

## Who does the work

You supply the experience and inspect the evidence. A writing assistant can help organize material and expose gaps, but it cannot establish that an event happened or that you caused an outcome. Check every proposed fact against a source you can examine.

Memory is a useful place to begin a draft. It is also fallible. Label recollection, distinguish estimates from recorded measurements, and avoid presenting unsupported precision as evidence.

## What this site provides

This site provides the method, a practical workbook, and a discussion of its limits. It does not provide an automated extraction service, a certification scheme, or machine-readable identity endpoints.

MootMoat is not an ATS bypass or an application-volume hack. It makes no promise of ranking, discovery, interviews, or hiring outcomes. Its immediate value is a record that a person can question, check, and revise.

## Start with one decision

Pick a project you can discuss appropriately. Complete one [workbook canvas](/docs/workbook/#start-with-a-common-record), then apply the [source and depth checks](/docs/doctrine/). Publish only what your evidence and disclosure permissions support.
